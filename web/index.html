Rescue access point

If the device cannot join your Wi-Fi (or has no saved network) it starts its own access point named sp500-setup-XXXXXX. The access key is shown on the device LCD. Connect your phone or computer to that network, then open http://192.168.4.1 (the Wi-Fi page opens automatically on most phones) and join your home network. After the device switches network this page may lose its connection: reconnect to your normal Wi-Fi and open http://sp500.local or the new IP address shown on the LCD.
